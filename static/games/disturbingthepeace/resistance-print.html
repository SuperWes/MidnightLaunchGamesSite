Print, cut, play.

Choose either PDF and print all four pages on two sheets of US Letter paper. Use 100% / actual size, portrait, double-sided with a long-edge flip. The backs are already mirrored. Test alignment on plain paper first, then cut along the shared card edges.

Each set includes 15 play cards, a Momentum tracker, a Resolve tracker, and a setup/play helper. Finished cards are 2.5 × 3.5 inches.
