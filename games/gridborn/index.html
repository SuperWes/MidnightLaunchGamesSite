GRIDBORN

A Spatial Grid-Based Deck-Builder Roguelike

Build your deck with powerful cards featuring directional attacks and shields as you navigate a treacherous hex grid map. Master the spatial combat system where positioning matters—chain element generation with powerful effects, apply devastating status effects, and use strategic card placement to overcome increasingly difficult enemies!

🎁 COMING SOON

🎮 Core Gameplay

  • Tactical 3x3 grid combat where positioning is everything
  • Directional attacks and shields—cards attack in 5 directions (forward, diagonals, sides)
  • 6 Element System: Generate and consume Fire, Ice, Lightning, Earth, Nature, and Poison for bonus damage
  • Navigate procedurally generated hex grid maps with multiple paths
  • Every run is unique with random encounters, rewards, and strategic choices

⚔️ Choose Your Archetype

6 Unique Playstyles:

  • ⚔️ Warrior - Pure physical combat with frontline pressure
  • 🔮 Caster - Fire/Ice magic with powerful elemental combos
  • 🗡️ Assassin - Poison/Nature specialist with flanking attacks
  • 🛡️ Paladin - Earth magic with defensive shield walls
  • 🌳 Druid - Nature magic with zone control and healing
  • ⚡ Stormcaller - Lightning magic with multi-target strikes

✨ Key Features

  • 121 Player Cards across 6 archetypes with unique abilities
  • 63 Enemy Cards with 7 dynamic archetype combinations
  • Strategic deck-building with shop and rest sites between battles
  • 7 Positioning Abilities: Frontline, Backline, Flanking, Zone Control, and more
  • Roguelike progression with strike system—three chances before game over
  • Boss encounters that test your deck-building skills
  • Artifact system for permanent bonuses and unique strategies
