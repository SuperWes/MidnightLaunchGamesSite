A Masquerade Mystery

Global Game Jam 2026

You are a journalist who has infiltrated the most exclusive masquerade ball of the season, hosted by the powerful Senator Peacock. Behind the glittering masks and champagne toasts lies a web of secrets, corruption, and danger.

Navigate treacherous conversations, gather evidence with your hidden camera, and uncover the truth before time runs out—or your cover is blown.
