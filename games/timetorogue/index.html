A Roguelike That Plays Out Across Your Day

A dungeon crawler built for Apple Watch, where the resource isn't mana or gold — it's real-world time. Every action spends focus and narrows your view; focus returns over hours. Descend as deep as you can before the dungeon catches you — even while the app is closed.

⌚ Coming soon to Apple Watch

Apple Watch · watchOS · Coming Soon

🎮 Core Gameplay

  • Tap to move, fight, and descend through a neon dungeon on your wrist
  • Every action spends focus — and your view narrows as it drains
  • Focus recovers over real-world time, so knowing when to walk away is the skill
  • Find the stairs down and dive deeper — the floors grow larger and more dangerous
  • Every run is different, and every death is permanent

⏳ What Makes It Different

Time is the resource

Play in glances across your day, not one long session. Focus recovers over hours — rest is a move, not a menu.

The maze moves while you're away

The dungeon takes its turns even when the app is closed. Leave yourself exposed and it can find you before you return.

Push your luck

Act on empty focus and you'll fight half-blind and take damage. Rest too long and the danger rises. Read the moment.

One rewind a day

A daily time-reverse lets you undo a single fatal move. Bank it, or spend it to save a deep run.

⌚ Built for Apple Watch

  • A watch complication shows your focus recovering at a glance
  • A nudge when you're rested and ready to dive again
  • Deepest-floor leaderboard with fresh monthly runs
  • Designed to be checked in seconds, anywhere
